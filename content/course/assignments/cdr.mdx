# Critical Design Review

## The Architectural Delta *(5 min)*

*Can your partner state, in one sentence, what bottleneck you are addressing and why the existing approach fails?
If they can't, your framing needs work.*

One sentence: Engineering the representation of data used to train a world model to handle sparse proximity images may increase sample efficiency of learning contact-dependent tasks.

One paragraph:
Predicting contact with nearby objects is difficult with model-based methods when the nearby objects are highly dynamic.
A proximity sensing world model that can dream future observations will be more robust to the dynamics found in data instead of explicit modeling.
By training a world model on sequences of low-dimensional proximity images instead of camera images, a contact avoidant policy can be trained effectively on a smaller distribution of trajectories since proximity is the salient feature for contact avoidance.

## The Loss Formulation *(5 min)*

*Derive your loss on paper for your partner.
They should follow from state-space and action-space definitions to $\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}$ without handwaving.*

*Reconstruction loss and vector quantization loss in the encoder/decoder:*

$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{VQ-VAE}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{recon}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{VQ}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{commitment}}
$$

The reconstruction loss promotes accurate reconstruction of the latent tokens.
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{recon}} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} \| x_i - \hat{x}_i \|^2
$$

The vector quantization loss promotes the latent tokens to be close to the codebook vectors.
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{VQ}} = \| \text{sg}[z_e(x)] - e \|_2^2
$$

The commitment loss promotes commitment to the codebook vectors.
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\text{commitment}} = \beta \| z_e(x) - \text{sg}[e] \|_2^2
$$

*Next-token prediction in the autoregressive transformer.*
The objective is to minimize the negative log-likelihood of the next latent token given all previous tokens.
For a single timestep $t$:

$$
\mathcal{L}_t = -\log p_\theta(z_{t+1} \mid z_{\leq t})
$$

The total loss is the average over the full sequence:

$$
\mathcal{L} = -\frac{1}{T-1} \sum_{t=1}^{T-1} \log p_\theta(z_{t+1} \mid z_{\leq t})
$$
